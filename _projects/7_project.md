I am interested in the energy impact of software systems and AI-assisted development workflows. My work investigates how refactoring affects software energy consumption, and how LLM-based development tools can be designed to balance correctness, safety, and energy efficiency. More broadly, I aim to build practical techniques for energy-aware software development and green AI for software engineering.
